Scientific Recommendation Logic

Agricultural yield depends upon matching crop biology with soil mechanics and local environmental cycles. This automated system evaluates three critical agro-climatic pillars:

Pillar 01
Soil Granulometry & Cation Exchange
Analyzes pore space, nutrient holding capacity, and root aeration across Alluvial (loamy silt), Black/Regur (high water retention), and Sandy beds.
Pillar 02
Seasonal Photoperiods & Temperature
Factors in vegetative vs. maturity thermal windows across monsoon seasons (Kharif), cold dry cycles (Rabi), and hot brief cycles (Zaid).
Pillar 03
Hydrological Balance & Water Thresholds
Prevents root-rot in drought-hardy plants and water-stress in thirsty staples by matching soil absorption with rainfall patterns.
