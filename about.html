Recommendation Logic & Agronomy

Agricultural productivity depends significantly on matching plant physiology with environmental conditions. This advisory engine optimizes for three key parameters:

1. Soil Granulometry & Chemistry
Examines aeration, nutrient holding capacity (CEC), and drainage attributes of Alluvial, Black, Clay, and Sandy soil beds.
2. Seasonal Photoperiods
Optimizes for crop photoperiod requirements across monsoon cycles (Kharif), winter cool cycles (Rabi), and hot transitional cycles (Zaid).
3. Hydrological Balance
Prevents root-rot or arid plant failure by aligning water intake thresholds with local availability.
