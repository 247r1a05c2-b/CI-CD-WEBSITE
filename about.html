About the Recommendation System

Maximizing agricultural yield requires aligning crop selection with regional agronomic parameters. This decision system evaluates three primary environmental factors:

1. Soil Texture & Properties
Evaluates moisture retention, drainage capacity, and root aeration across Alluvial, Black/Regur, Clayey, and Sandy soils.
2. Seasonal Photoperiod (Cropping Cycle)
Accounts for monsoon crops (Kharif), winter cool-season crops (Rabi), and short summer crops (Zaid).
3. Hydrological Availability
Considers baseline precipitation and irrigation capacity to avoid water-stress or water-logging.
