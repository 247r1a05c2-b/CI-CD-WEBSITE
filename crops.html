Crop Reference Database

Complete catalog of 16 agro-climatic crops categorized by soil conditions, season, and rainfall requirements.

🌾
Rice (Oryza Sativa)
Kharif • High Water

Optimal in clayey alluvial soils with continuous moisture. Requires standing water and warm conditions (22°C–32°C).

🍞
Wheat (Triticum)
Rabi • Moderate Water

Needs cool growing temperatures (10°C–15°C) and warm ripening weather. Well-suited to loamy alluvial soil beds.

🌽
Maize (Zea Mays)
Kharif • Moderate Water

Versatile food and fodder grain that thrives in well-drained loamy to sandy soils with abundant sunshine.

🧵
Cotton (Gossypium)
Kharif • Black Soil

Major commercial fiber crop requiring deep, moisture-retentive black regur soils and 210+ frost-free days.

🎋
Sugarcane
Perennial • High Water

Long-duration crop needing tropical warmth, high humidity, fertile alluvial soil, and sustained irrigation.

🫘
Soybean
Kharif • Moderate Water

High-protein legume cultivated extensively in fertile black soils; fixes atmospheric nitrogen to boost soil fertility.

🌻
Sunflower
Zaid / Kharif • Drought Hardy

Adaptable oilseed performing well across diverse soils, needing light-to-moderate moisture and full solar exposure.

🥣
Chickpeas (Gram)
Rabi • Low Water

Crucial pulse crop thriving in mild winter temperatures and well-aerated sandy loam or clay loam soils.

🥜
Groundnut (Peanut)
Kharif • Sandy Loam

Requires loose, sandy-textured soils to enable easy subterranean peg penetration and pod development.

🌼
Mustard
Rabi • Moderate Water

Major cool-season oilseed thriving in loamy soils across sub-tropical climates with clear sunny days.

🌾
Pearl Millet (Bajra)
Kharif • Arid Resilient

Extremely resilient dryland cereal capable of producing reliable yields in nutrient-poor, sandy desert soils.

🥣
Sorghum (Jowar)
Kharif/Rabi • Low Water

Drought-tolerant dual-purpose crop (grain and fodder) that flourishes in semi-arid black and clayey soils.

🍉
Watermelon
Zaid • Warm Climate

Fast-maturing summer crop requiring long warm days, dry weather during ripening, and well-drained sandy riverbeds.

☕
Coffee (Arabica / Robusta)
Plantation • High Altitude

Requires shade canopy, well-drained volcanic or red laterite soils, and steady rainfall on undulating hill slopes.

🍵
Tea (Camellia Sinensis)
Plantation • High Rainfall

Thrives in acidic, organic-rich soil on sloped terrain to ensure excellent water drainage without water-logging.

🥔
Potato
Rabi • Loose Loam

Requires friable, loose loamy soil rich in organic matter and moderate cool weather for optimal tuber expansion.
